Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 9, 2026 • Last Updated: February 9, 2026

At ionXtech, we build digital products with a privacy-first philosophy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and protect your information across our website (ionxtech.in), digital services, Google OAuth integrations, and mobile applications.

Effective Date: February 9, 2026

ionXtech (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your personal data and upholding absolute transparency in our software engineering and authentication practices. This document describes the types of information we collect, how it is handled, and your rights concerning your personal data.

1. Privacy-First Principles

We believe technology should serve users without compromising their fundamental right to privacy:

  • Minimal Data Collection: We only collect data that is strictly necessary to authenticate your identity or provide requested services.
  • Zero Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.
  • On-Device Processing: Where feasible, our utility applications operate locally on your device without sending your content to remote servers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with ionXtech, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Direct Inquiries & Waitlist Submissions: When you submit a form on our website (such as our contact form or domain/hosting waitlists), we receive the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, topic of interest, and message content.
  • Google OAuth Authentication Data: When you choose to sign in or authenticate using Google OAuth (“Sign in with Google”) on ionXtech platforms or services, we receive the basic public profile information permitted by your consent:
    • Your primary email address
    • Your full name and display name
    • Your profile avatar image URL
    • Your unique Google Account identifier
    We never access, request, or store your Google Account password, private contacts, emails, or any Google services outside the explicit authorization dialog shown by Google.
  • Technical & Log Data: When accessing our website, standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and timestamp) may be temporarily captured by our edge security infrastructure (Cloudflare) for DDoS protection and malicious traffic filtering.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain secure login sessions across ionXtech web applications and consoles.
  • To provide, operate, and maintain requested cloud, domain, and application services.
  • To respond to your inquiries, support requests, and billing communications.
  • To detect, prevent, and mitigate fraud, spam, or security incidents on our infrastructure.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Google API Services Compliance Notice

ionXtech’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In accordance with Google’s Limited Use requirements:

  • We strictly limit our use of Google user data to providing or improving user-facing features that are prominent in the requesting application’s user interface.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties unless necessary to provide or improve these features, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with explicit user notice.
  • We do not use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless we have obtained your affirmative agreement for specific messages or diagnostic troubleshooting, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required by law.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Trusted third-party infrastructure providers that enable our services (such as Cloudflare for DDoS protection and edge delivery) process technical metadata under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by valid legal process, subpoena, or applicable regulatory law, we may disclose information strictly necessary to satisfy statutory obligations.

6. Data Retention and Security

All communications between your device and ionXtech services are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data stored in our database systems is protected by strict access controls and encryption at rest. We retain personal data only for as long as your account is active or as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes outlined in this policy.

7. Your Rights, Data Deletion, and Revoking Permissions

You have full control over your personal information and third-party integrations:

  • Revoking Google OAuth Access: You can revoke ionXtech’s access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Requesting Data Deletion: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data. To initiate a deletion request, please email our privacy team at privacy@ionxtech.in or hello@ionxtech.in with the subject “Data Deletion Request”. We will confirm and execute the permanent purge of your data within 30 days.
