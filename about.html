About ionXtech

We are a product-focused technology company building fast, local-first utility applications and modern web infrastructure.

Software That Respects Your Time and Privacy

At ionXtech, we believe everyday digital utilities should do their job immediately without bloated interfaces, dark patterns, or invasive cloud tracking. Our Android applications (QR Solutions, Privacy Display, Auto Call Scheduler, and LED Scroller) are trusted by more than 165,000 users worldwide because they prioritize speed, offline reliability, and local data ownership.

As we grow, we are bringing that same minimalist engineering discipline to developer and startup infrastructure across five divisions: ionXtech, ionXapps, ionXhosting, ionXdomains, and ionXservices.
