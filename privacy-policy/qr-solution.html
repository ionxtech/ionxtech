Privacy Policy: QR Solutions

Effective Date: February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Solutions (developed by ionXtech) handles permissions and data on your Android device.

1. Camera Permission

This application requires access to your device's camera strictly to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera frames are processed locally in device memory and no images or video feeds are transmitted to our servers.

2. Local Data Storage

Scanned and generated QR code history is stored locally on your device for your convenience. We do not track, collect, or upload your scanning activity to external servers. You can clear your history at any time from within the application or by clearing app storage in Android Settings.

3. Third-Party Services

If advertisements or crash diagnostics are enabled in your store version, standard Google Play Services SDKs may process basic device identifiers in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
