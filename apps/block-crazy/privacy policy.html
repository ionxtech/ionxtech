Welcome to Block Craze (“Block Puzzle”, “the Game”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), developed by Maheen Shaikh and published by ionXtech. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information that may be collected while you enjoy our game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you regarding your data when using Block Craze on Android devices.

By installing and playing Block Craze, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in a player-first, privacy-respecting experience:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to play the game.
  • No Sensitive Personal Information: We do not collect or request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise GPS location.
  • No Financial Data: Block Craze does not collect credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank details. All gameplay features are free to access.

3. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

While the core game runs locally on your device and does not transmit personal data to our own servers, the Game integrates third-party services (such as advertising networks) that may automatically collect certain device and diagnostic data.

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Block Craze uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display optional rewarded ads (e.g., watching an ad to revive a game session).

Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / Android Ad ID), device model, operating system version, and mobile network information.
  • IP Address: Used to determine coarse (approximate/city-level) geographic location for ad relevance and fraud prevention.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Information about ads served, viewed, tapped, or dismissed, and frequency of ad impressions.
  • Diagnostics and Performance: App crash logs, responsiveness metrics, and SDK performance data.

For more information on how Google collects and uses data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Local Data Stored on Your Device

To provide a seamless gaming experience, Block Craze stores certain game progress locally on your device using standard Android local storage (localStorage / internal app storage):

  • High scores and best scores per game mode
  • Unlocked visual themes and user display preferences
  • Daily challenge completion timestamps and attempt counts
  • Sound and vibration settings

Note: This data remains strictly on your local device. It is never uploaded to any external server and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app’s cache/data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the Game.

5. How We Use the Information

The information collected by third-party SDKs is used solely for:

  1. Ad Delivery & Monetization: Serving age-appropriate ads and rewarding players who opt to view rewarded continue videos.
  2. Fraud Prevention & Security: Preventing invalid ad traffic, bot activities, and abusive behavior.
  3. App Performance & Stability: Monitoring technical performance, identifying crashes, and ensuring smooth frame rates.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security and Transmission

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • All communications between third-party SDKs and their servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS / TLS).
  • We follow modern Google Play security guidelines, targeting the latest Android SDK versions (Android API 36) with secure defaults.

7. Your Privacy Rights and Ad Choices

You have full control over your privacy on your Android device:

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or toggle Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Once opted out, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual rather than personalized based on your interests.

European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of data held by third-party ad networks, and to manage your consent preferences via in-app consent prompts.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you reside in California, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising (which you can exercise via the device-level opt-out above).
