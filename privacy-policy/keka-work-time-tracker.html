Privacy Policy: Keka Work Time Tracker

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keka Work Time Tracker (a Google Chrome extension developed by ionXtech) handles user data and browser permissions.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Keka Work Time Tracker does not collect, log, store, transmit, or share any personal information, employee details, login credentials, attendance records, or browsing history. We do not operate any backend servers or external databases for this extension.

2. 100% Local Browser Computation

All work duration calculations, break deductions, and daily target countdowns happen strictly inside your local Google Chrome browser session. The extension reads timestamps displayed on your open Keka HR portal tab in local memory to compute your remaining work hours in real time.

3. Browser Permissions Explained

  • Local Storage: Used solely to store your personal shift duration preferences and display settings locally inside your browser.
  • Host Access (Keka Portal): Used strictly to read attendance punch-in and punch-out timestamps on the Keka attendance page when you view your dashboard.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

The extension contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising scripts, and zero tracking pixels. The entire codebase is open source and publicly auditable on GitHub at github.com/ARSN72/WorkHour.
