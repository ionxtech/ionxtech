Privacy Policy: Auto Call Scheduler

Last Updated: April 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Auto Call Scheduler (Call & Message Scheduler) mobile application developed by ionXtech collects, uses, and protects information when you use our app. Your privacy is important to us and we are committed to full transparency about our data practices.

Auto Call Scheduler lets you schedule automatic phone calls at a date and time you choose. The app runs primarily on your device. Below we describe every category of data that may be accessed or processed.

1. Data We Collect

Most data accessed by the application never leaves your device. The only information that may be transmitted off-device is limited to optional, ephemerally-processed analytics and crash diagnostics.

a) Scheduled Call Data (Stored Locally, Never Transmitted)

Phone numbers, scheduled dates, and times you enter are stored exclusively in your device's local application storage. This data is never sent to ionXtech or any third party.

b) Contacts (Optional, On-Device Only)

If you choose to pick a number from your contacts, the app reads that phone number on-device solely to populate the scheduling form. No contact list data is transmitted off your device or stored externally.

c) Analytics Data (Optional, Processed Ephemerally)

To understand how users interact with the app (such as feature usage and session stability), the app may collect anonymised usage analytics:

  • This data is processed ephemerally, held in memory only for as long as needed to service the real-time request and retained no longer.
  • Collection is optional. You can opt out at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • No personally identifiable information (name, phone number, contact data) is included in analytics events.

d) Crash Logs & Diagnostics (Optional, Processed Ephemerally)

If the app encounters an unexpected error, diagnostic information (device model, OS version, stack trace) may be transmitted to help us identify and fix bugs:

  • Crash data is processed ephemerally and used strictly to diagnose the specific issue.
  • Collection is optional and can be disabled from the app's Settings screen.
  • Crash logs never include contact data or scheduled call numbers.

2. Permissions Used

The application requests the following Android permissions:

  • CALL_PHONE: Allows the app to initiate a phone call automatically at the scheduled time. Required for core functionality.
  • READ_CONTACTS (Optional): Allows you to pick a contact's number from your address book when setting up a call. Only the selected number is used on-device.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Allows the app to trigger the scheduled call at the exact minute you configure.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Allows the app to restore pending scheduled calls after a device restart so your schedules are not lost.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Scheduled call data is stored exclusively in your device's local storage and is deleted when you remove the scheduled entry or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash diagnostic data is processed ephemerally and is not retained beyond real-time processing.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following standard Google LLC services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: Optional crash diagnostics (firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: Optional anonymised usage statistics (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Google AdMob: Displays in-app advertisements and may use standard device advertising identifiers (support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

6. User Choice & Opt-Out

  • Disable analytics and crash reporting at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • Opt out of personalised ads via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Revoke the READ_CONTACTS permission at any time in Android Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored scheduling data.

7. Security & Children's Privacy

All off-device diagnostic or ad requests are encrypted via HTTPS. This application is not directed toward children under the age of 13.
