Privacy Policy: LED Scroller

Effective Date: February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LED Scroller (developed by ionXtech) handles user input and third-party services.

1. Information Collection and Local Text Rendering

The LED Scroller application does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. Any text or message you type into the banner display is processed strictly locally in your device's memory and is never transmitted to ionXtech servers.

2. Third-Party Advertisements

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the free version of the app. AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant advertisements in accordance with your device's ad privacy settings.

3. Permissions

LED Scroller does not request access to sensitive permissions such as your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.
